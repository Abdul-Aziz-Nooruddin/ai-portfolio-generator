Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: October 3, 2026 • Effective Date: October 3, 2026

1. The 24-Hour Try-Before-You-Buy Model

At MyFolio, we believe you should never pay for something without knowing exactly what it looks and feels like. We provide 3 free evaluation builds every week. Each evaluation build remains active for 24 hours with full Web Studio customization and desktop/mobile 3D WebGL previewing.

During this 24-hour evaluation window, you can inspect your layout, verify project narratives, test color tokens, and ensure complete satisfaction before committing to any paid plan.

2. One-Time Builds (Lifetime Starter — ₹149)

What is included: 1 permanent build deployed to a Netlify address, zero watermarks, and immediate access to full standalone source code (.ZIP download).

Because the Lifetime Starter plan delivers immediate digital access to complete standalone source code and incurs immediate third-party infrastructure deployment costs, purchases are generally final once the source code ZIP has been downloaded or permanent deployment has completed.

Technical Failure Exceptions: If our platform fails to deploy your portfolio, or if you experience a verified technical defect that our engineering team cannot resolve within 48 hours of reporting, you are entitled to a 100% full refund within 7 days of purchase.

3. Pro Custom Domain Subscriptions (₹149 / Month)

What is included: Your branded custom vanity subdomain (<username>.myfolio.tech), zero watermarks, full source code export, priority CDN routing, and automated GitHub synchronization.

Cancellation Anytime: You may cancel your Pro Domain monthly subscription at any time without fees or penalties directly from your User Dashboard or by sending an email to support@myfolio.tech.

Upon cancellation:

  • Your subscription will not renew at the end of your current paid billing period.
  • Your branded custom subdomain will remain active and published until the end of your current paid billing cycle.
  • You retain full rights to your exported source code ZIP indefinitely, allowing you to self-host your website on any hosting service (Vercel, Netlify, Cloudflare, AWS, or GitHub Pages) with zero lock-in.

4. Grace Period & Data Deletion Policy

If a monthly subscription renews unsuccessfully or lapses:

  • 5-Day Grace Period: We provide a 5-day grace period during which we send reminders and attempt payment reconciliation. Your custom subdomain remains intact during this window.
  • Lapsed State: After 5 days of unpaid status, the live subdomain route is unmounted and replaced with an expiration notice.
  • Complete Account Deletion: You may request permanent deletion of your account and all associated profile data at any time via your Profile settings or by emailing support@myfolio.tech. Once triggered, all data, repository extracts, and build artifacts are irreversibly purged.

5. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund under the technical failure policy or report a billing discrepancy:

  1. Email support@myfolio.tech with the subject line Refund Request - [Your Order ID].
  2. Include your registered email address, payment transaction ID (from Razorpay or card statement), and a brief description of the technical issue.
  3. Our team will review and respond within 24–48 business hours. Approved refunds are credited back to the original payment method within 5–7 business days depending on your bank.
