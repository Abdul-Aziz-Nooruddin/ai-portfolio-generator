Frequently Asked Questions & Takedown Rules

Rules regarding 24h preview lifecycles, 5-day grace periods, account deletion, and copyright takedowns.

1. Evaluation Previews & Lifecycle Schedulers

Q How does the 24-hour preview evaluation work?
When you connect your GitHub handle or upload your resume, MyFolio synthesizes an interactive 3D WebGL website. You receive 3 free builds every week. Each evaluation build remains active on our temporary staging servers for 24 hours. During this window, you can view your site on desktop and mobile and customize it in Web Studio.
Q What happens after the 24 hours expire?
After 24 hours without an active subscription or one-time build purchase, the temporary live link transitions to preview lapsed status. Visitors to the URL will see an expiration status notice. Your design state remains securely stored in your dashboard for 5 days in case you wish to unlock it.
Q What is the 5-day grace period?
If a monthly subscription payment is declined or a preview remains unpaid, we maintain a 5-day grace period. During this period, we send email reminders so you have time to update billing credentials. If no action is taken after 5 days, our automated lifecycle engine permanently purges the generated site directory and build artifacts from our servers to protect storage hygiene.

2. Source Code Export & Platform Independence

Q Do I own the code generated by MyFolio?
Yes, 100%. Unlike proprietary website builders (Wix, Squarespace, Webflow) that trap your data, every paid build on MyFolio includes a clean standalone source code export (ZIP download). You own the HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and WebGL shader code completely with zero vendor lock-in. You may self-host it on Vercel, Netlify, Cloudflare Pages, AWS, or any server.
Q Are watermarks removed after payment?
Yes. Once you complete checkout for either the ₹149 Lifetime Starter or the ₹149/mo Pro Domain plan, all preview watermarks and evaluation banners are completely stripped from both the live hosted deployment and the exported ZIP source code.

3. Account Deletion & Data Erasure

Q How do I delete my account and all personal data?
You have the right to complete erasure at any time:
  • Self-Service: Navigate to Identity & Control Room (/profile) and click Delete Account.
  • Automated Execution: This triggers an immediate, irreversible cascade purge: your account records, session tokens, design briefs, parsed resume text, uploaded avatars, and cloud hosting sites are erased within seconds.
  • Email Request: You can also email support@myfolio.tech with the subject Account Deletion Request.
Q Does MyFolio sell my data to third parties?
Never. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your resume, GitHub repositories, or contact credentials. Your repository data is processed strictly in-memory and through encrypted API calls to generate your personal portfolio.

4. Copyright, DMCA & Content Takedown Rules

Q What should I do if a portfolio contains my copyrighted work or unauthorized personal information?
MyFolio respects intellectual property and identity privacy. If you believe a portfolio published on *.myfolio.tech infringes upon your copyright, uses your likeness without authorization, or contains sensitive personal data, you may submit an expedited takedown notice.
Q What information is required in a Takedown Notice?
To file a valid takedown notice, email takedown@myfolio.tech or support@myfolio.tech with:
  • The exact URL of the offending portfolio (e.g. https://username.myfolio.tech or https://myfolio.tech/p/siteId).
  • Identification of the copyrighted work, personal likeness, or trademark claimed to be infringed, or proof of identity.
  • Sufficient contact information (name, address, telephone number, and email address).
  • A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the disputed use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  • A statement made under penalty of perjury that the information in the notification is accurate and that you are authorized to act.
Q How fast does MyFolio respond to takedown requests?
Upon receiving a bona fide takedown notice, our security operations team investigates and restricts or removes public access to the contested URL within 24 to 48 business hours. If the infringement is severe (e.g. leaked private credentials or doxxing), immediate emergency unmounting is executed.
