Overview

MyFolio (“we”, “us”, “our”) operates the myfolio.tech platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our service.

By using MyFolio, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you disagree with any part of this policy, please do not use our service.

What we collect

We collect information you provide directly, information from connected services (GitHub, Google), and basic usage analytics. Specifically:

  • Account information: Name, email address, profile picture (from Google OAuth)
  • GitHub data: Public repository metadata, commit history, README content — only what’s publicly available
  • Resume content: Text you paste or upload for portfolio generation (processed and stored only for your builds)
  • Usage data: Pages visited, features used, build counts (anonymised, via GA4)
  • Payment data: Handled entirely by Razorpay — we never store card details

How we use it

Your data is used solely to provide and improve the MyFolio service:

  • Generating your 3D portfolio from GitHub and resume data
  • Hosting your portfolio on our servers and CDN
  • Sending transactional emails (build confirmations, account updates)
  • Improving the generation engine based on aggregate, anonymised usage patterns

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We share data only with:

  • Netlify / Cloudflare: Hosting infrastructure for your published portfolio
  • Supabase: Database and auth provider (EU region)
  • Razorpay: Payment processing (PCI DSS compliant)
  • Google Analytics: Anonymised usage analytics

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. Go to Account → Profile to update your information, or email us at privacy@myfolio.tech to request complete data deletion.
