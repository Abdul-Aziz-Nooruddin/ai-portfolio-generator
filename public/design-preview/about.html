About MyFolio

We think your work deserves better than a template.

MyFolio exists because the standard portfolio is broken. Static pages, buried in a sea of identical Canva exports. We built something different.

Build yours free →
The idea

Built by a developer,
for developers.

MyFolio was born out of frustration. After spending hours tweaking yet another theme-based portfolio, the question was simple: why does this have to be so hard?

Your GitHub already has everything — the projects, the commits, the evidence. We just needed to build the engine that translates that into something spatial, interactive, and worth showing.

Today, MyFolio generates fully personalised Three.js WebGL portfolio worlds from your actual data. No templates in the bad sense — every output is shaped by what you’ve actually built.
