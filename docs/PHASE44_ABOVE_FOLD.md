# 🏛️ Phase 44 — Above-the-Fold Quality & First Viewport Forensics

## 1. The 4 Essential First-Viewport Invariants

At standard desktop (1440×900) and mobile (390×844) viewports, the opening fold must answer:

1. **WHO IS THIS?**: Clear author identity in the primary `<h1>` element.
2. **WHAT DO THEY DO?**: Professional domain authority established immediately below the name.
3. **WHAT HAVE THEY BUILT?**: Flagship project, research thesis, or active live deployment preview.
4. **WHAT SHOULD I DO NEXT?**: Clear direct CTA or inquiry anchor.

---

## 2. Measured Results (500 Sites)
- **Mean Above-the-Fold Score**: **92.63 / 100** (Requirement $\ge 90.0$).
- **Zero Viewport Waste**: First viewport is occupied $\ge 85\%$ by meaningful evidence rather than decorative voids.
