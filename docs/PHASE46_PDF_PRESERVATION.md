# 🏛️ Phase 46 PDF & Resume Evidence Preservation

## 1. Multi-Page & Column Preservation
The resume parsing pipeline handles single-page and multi-page documents, tabular layouts, and multi-column formats:

- **Employment History**: Role titles, organization names, duration periods, narrative descriptions, discrete responsibilities, and quantitative achievements.
- **Academic Background**: Degree designations, institutions, completion years, specialized coursework, and doctoral honors.
- **Measurable Outcomes**: Explicit revenue, latency, and throughput metrics are tied directly to their respective career nodes.

---

## 2. Retention Invariants
- Second-column text is never lost.
- Bullet point arrays survive intact as structured lists.
- Coursework and dissertation awards are preserved and rendered.
