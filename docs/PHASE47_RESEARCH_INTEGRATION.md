# 🏛️ Phase 47 Research & Publication Artifact Integration

## Academic Artifact Treatment
Publications are treated as peer-reviewed scientific artifacts rather than standard software cards:
- **DOI & Indexing**: Direct hyperlinks with formatted `[PUB_01] DOI: ...` headers.
- **Abstract Callout**: Styled with left accent border and readable academic leading.
- **Methodology & Findings**: Explicit bolded sections detailing empirical hardware benchmarks, TLA+ state exploration, and speedup proofs.
