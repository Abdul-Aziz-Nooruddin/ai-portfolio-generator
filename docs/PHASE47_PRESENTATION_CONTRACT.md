# 🏛️ Phase 47 Content Presentation Strategy Contract

## Presentation Strategies
`ContentPresentationContract` selects layout treatments tailored to evidence depth:

1. **`HERO_EVIDENCE`**: Primary candidate identity, thesis, and core CTAs.
2. **`ARCHITECTURE_VIEW`**: Structural patterns, concurrency models, and runtime topologies.
3. **`METRIC_CALLOUT`**: Measured benchmarks, speedups, and revenue numbers.
4. **`TECHNICAL_SPEC`**: Engineering challenges, decisions, and trade-offs.
5. **`TIMELINE`**: Narrative career history with responsibilities and outcomes.
6. **`RESEARCH_DOSSIER`**: Academic papers with venue, DOI, abstract, methodology, and findings.
7. **`CUSTOM_EVIDENCE`**: Patents, grants, governance, testbeds, and custom metadata cards.
