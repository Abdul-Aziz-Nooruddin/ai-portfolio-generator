# 🏛️ Phase 48 Questionnaire & Philosophy Integration

## Natural Narrative Presentation
Questionnaire answers (e.g. core engineering philosophy, career highlights, future research visions) are transformed into intentional editorial thesis statements.

- **No Raw Q&A Forms**: Answers are never rendered as raw numbered quiz items (`Question 1: ...`).
- **Semantic Callouts**: Integrated into the hero thesis, personal statement, or lateral context rails.
