# 🏛️ Phase 44 — Human Perception & Physical Layout Quality

## 1. The 9 Perception Dimensions

1. **First Impression (15 pts)**: Instant clarity of author identity, core engineering domain, and immediate visual confidence.
2. **Scanability & Heading Hierarchy (15 pts)**: Single clear `h1`, structured `h2` chapters, clean reading measures.
3. **Spatial Quality (10 pts)**: Standardized fluid `--section-gap` pacing, balanced whitespace.
4. **Typography (10 pts)**: Coherent heading/body pairing, fluid clamp scaling, comfortable `1.65` line height.
5. **Component Quality (10 pts)**: Absence of card monopolies, diverse artifact presentations.
6. **Content-to-Space Fit (10 pts)**: Focused containers for sparse evidence, multi-zone grids for rich evidence.
7. **Mobile Quality (10 pts)**: Responsive viewport metadata, zero horizontal overflow.
8. **Accessibility (10 pts)**: Semantic landmarks (`<main>`, `<header>`, `<footer>`), `prefers-reduced-motion` blocks.
9. **Performance (10 pts)**: Clean DOM tree depth (<250 nodes), zero blocking scripts.
