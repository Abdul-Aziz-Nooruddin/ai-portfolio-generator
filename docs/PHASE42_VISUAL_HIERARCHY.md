# 🏛️ Phase 42 — Visual Hierarchy Forensics

## 1. Hierarchy Dimensions Evaluated

1. **Heading-to-Body Contrast**: `clamp(2.4rem, 5.5vw, 4.2rem)` on `h1` vs `1.05rem` body text establishes a distinct primary anchor.
2. **First-Viewport Answerability**: Above-the-fold region immediately answers:
   - *Who is the author?*
   - *What is their primary domain?*
   - *What is their flagship achievement?*
   - *Where can I inspect the code/live demo?*
3. **Primary vs Secondary Prominence**: Flagship projects receive prominent typography, architecture callouts, and live links; tech stack matrices and timestamps remain subordinate reference material.
4. **CTA Discoverability**: Direct anchors (`View Live ↗`, `GitHub Repo ↗`, `Direct Inquiries ↗`) are highlighted with high-contrast accent colors and standard keyboard-focusable HTML elements.
