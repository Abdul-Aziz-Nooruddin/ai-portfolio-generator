# 🏛️ Phase 44 — Forensic Rendered Reality Audit

## 1. Executive Summary: The Governing Law

$$\text{INTERNAL DESIGN INTENT IS NOT PROOF OF EXTERNAL DESIGN QUALITY.}$$

A portfolio can have a unique topology, a valid perceptual fingerprint, and an evidence-driven decision trace, yet still fail in the physical browser viewport if:
- components repeat identically across different page wireframes,
- first viewports fail to communicate identity clearly,
- mobile widths suffer from awkward reflow,
- or decorative flourishes overwhelm real achievements.

---

## 2. Forensic Resolution in Phase 44
1. **Rendered Reality Model (`RenderedRealityModel`)**: Analyzes physical container bounds, section gaps, heading structures, and whitespace geometry directly from rendered markup.
2. **Rendered Quality Scoring (`RenderedQualityScore`)**: Evaluates 9 human perception dimensions: First Impression, Scanability, Spatial Quality, Typography, Component Quality, Content-to-Space Fit, Mobile Quality, Accessibility, and Performance.
3. **Component Convergence Auditing (`ComponentConvergenceDetector`)**: Enforces $\le 15.0\%$ component collision rate, ensuring that card shapes, CTA buttons, and project items do not homogenize.
4. **Above-the-Fold Forensics (`AboveFoldAnalyzer`)**: Ensures first viewports immediately answer: *Who is this? What do they do? What have they built? What should I do next?*
5. **Anti-AI Website Smell Detection (`AiTemplateSmellDetector`)**: Eliminates generic clichés ("Hi, I'm...", filler slogans, arbitrary skill progress bars).
