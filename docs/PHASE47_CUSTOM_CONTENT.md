# 🏛️ Phase 47 Custom & Unmodeled Evidence Presentation

## Dynamic Specification Presentation
Custom and unmodeled fields are rendered in styled specification blocks without being dumped into unstructured text piles:
- **Patents & IP**: Formatted with patent numbers and invention scopes.
- **Grants & Governance**: Formatted with funding amounts and working group positions.
- **Keynote Talks**: Formatted with conference names and lecture titles.
- **Hardware Facilities**: Formatted with cluster specs and networking details.
