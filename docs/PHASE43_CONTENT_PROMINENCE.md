# 🏛️ Phase 43 — Content Prominence & Critical-Field Visual Prominence

## 1. Critical Evidence Prominence Rule

Every `CRITICAL` and `IMPORTANT` field identified by `ContentImportanceModel` and `DesignCausalityGraph` must receive physical DOM prominence:

$$\text{Critical-Field Visual Prominence} \ge 95.0\%$$

- **Flagship Achievements**: Positioned in primary viewports with larger typographic scales (`clamp(2.4rem, 5vw, 4rem)`).
- **Verified Metrics**: Emphasized in high-contrast data rows or badge callouts.
- **Architectural Diagrams / Specs**: Given full-width containers rather than compressed sub-cards.
- **Secondary References**: Stack badges, star counts, and dates are placed into subordinate reading layers.
