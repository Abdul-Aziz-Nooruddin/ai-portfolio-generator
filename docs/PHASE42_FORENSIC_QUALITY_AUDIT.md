# 🏛️ Phase 42 — Forensic Quality Audit

## 1. Executive Summary

Phase 41 successfully addressed geometric and topological diversity. However, diversity alone is not sufficient; a generator can produce 50 distinct layouts that are all mediocre.

Phase 42 shifts the core optimization target to:
$$\text{Distinct Visual Solutions} + \text{Human Design Excellence} = \text{Award-Winning Generative Portfolios}$$

---

## 2. Forensic Diagnosis of Pre-Phase 42 Quality Flaws

1. **Content Subordination to Layout**:
   - Previously, portfolios selected a template or topology and forced developer evidence into that layout's arbitrary slots.
   - For research scientists, groundbreaking papers were buried beneath generic skill matrices.
   - For full-stack engineers with deep distributed architecture, system designs were flattened into identical text cards.
2. **First-Viewport Ambiguity**:
   - The opening fold frequently lacked a clear, scannable answer to: *Who is this? What do they build? Where is the proof?*
3. **Uniform Density Assumption**:
   - Sparse profiles (e.g. junior developers with 2 projects) were rendered in layouts designed for 10 projects, leaving artificial empty voids.
   - Rich profiles (e.g. principal architects with metrics, publications, and patents) were compressed into standard card grids.

---

## 3. Phase 42 Architectural Fixes

- **`ContentImportanceModel`**: Evaluates evidence strength, uniqueness, and measurable impact to classify fields into `CRITICAL`, `IMPORTANT`, `SUPPORTING`, `SECONDARY`, and `DECORATIVE`.
- **`ContentHierarchy`**: Builds an evidence-driven narrative arc: `PRIMARY STORY -> SECONDARY STORY -> SUPPORTING PROOF -> REFERENCE MATERIAL`.
- **`ProjectQualityModel`**: Maps project artifacts to authentic presentational depths (`TECHNICAL_DOSSIER`, `CASE_STUDY`, `METRICS_TELEMETRY`, `RESEARCH_ARTIFACT`).
- **`InformationDensityModel`**: Balances cognitive density dynamically, preventing underdesign on sparse profiles and overdesign on rich profiles.
- **`HumanQualityScore` & Quality Gate**: Evaluates 15 human-centered design dimensions, requiring $\ge 85/100$ mean quality.
