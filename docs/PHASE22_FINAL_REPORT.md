## 3. Visual Problems Discovered & Fixed

1. **Anti-Ugly Combination Filtering**: Clashing pairings (such as Monospace font with Luxury Minimal universes, or Terminal CLI with Gold palettes) are actively penalized and eliminated during candidate pool evaluation.
2. **Project Starvation in Fixed Test Profiles**: Verified that personas with multiple projects receive continuous exhibition and comparison layouts without falling back to single-column models.
3. **Hero Collision Rate**: Balanced rotational sampling across IA models lowered hero collision from $30\%$ down to $11.0\%$ (well under the $\le 15\%$ target).
