# 🏛️ Phase 32: Product UX & Design Constitution Architecture

## 1. Executive Summary

Phase 32 establishes a strict visual and functional separation between:
1. **The Generator Product UI**: A calm, editorial, tactile, typography-led tool designed to inspire trust and clarity.
2. **The Generated Portfolios**: 10 distinct visual universes and 10 macro information architecture models designed specifically for each creator's discipline.

## 3. Product User Journey

1. **Discovery (5s)**: Headline clearly communicates value: *"Turn your work into a portfolio worth remembering"*.
2. **Input Selection**: Tabs allow starting with GitHub, Resume (PDF), Images, Guided Questions, or Combining all sources.
3. **Generation Progress (Honest 7 Stages)**:
   - 01: Understanding your work
   - 02: Organizing your story
   - 03: Choosing a visual direction
   - 04: Designing the page
   - 05: Shaping your projects
   - 06: Checking the result
   - 07: Preparing your portfolio
   - Live elapsed wall-clock timer display.
4. **Fullscreen Studio Preview**:
   - Canvas dominates viewport with responsive switcher (Desktop, Tablet 768px, Mobile 390px).
   - Humanized customizer drawer for spacing, corner radius, typography scale, motion intensity, and section ordering.
5. **Static ZIP Export**: Standalone offline package without backend watermarks or development URLs.
