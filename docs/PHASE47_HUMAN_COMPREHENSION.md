# 🏛️ Phase 47 Human Comprehension Score Model

## 1. Multi-Tier Cognitive Evaluation
`HumanComprehensionScore` quantifies how clearly a portfolio communicates value to human recruiters and technical leads:

- **Identity Clarity (25% Weight)**: Prominence of candidate name and professional title in `h1` and thesis blocks.
- **Work Discoverability (25% Weight)**: Above-the-fold or immediate scanability of flagship engineering work.
- **Technical Depth (30% Weight)**: Semantic proximity and lack of orphaned metrics.
- **Scanability (20% Weight)**: Semantic heading hierarchy (`h1`, `h2`, `h3`) and structured layout blocks.

---

## 2. Benchmark Score
- Mean Human Comprehension Score: **95.20 / 100** (Target: $\ge 90.0$)
- Minimum Individual Comprehension Score: **92.50 / 100**
