# 🏛️ Phase 35: Black & White Structural Visual Audit

## 1. Principle: The Black & White Test for Structural Truth

> *"A human looking at 20 generated portfolios in black-and-white should recognize multiple genuinely different information architectures, not 20 color variants of the same wireframe."*

When vibrant gradients, glowing neon borders, and colorful hero backgrounds are stripped away (`filter: grayscale(100%) contrast(120%)`), cosmetic styling cannot hide structural monotony.

In Phase 35, portfolios are audited directly in high-contrast monochrome mode to ensure that:
1. **Container Physics differ radically**: fixed sidebars vs 860px reading monograph vs full-bleed runways vs 3-column magazine spreads.
2. **Whitespace distribution creates distinct visual rhythms**: dense monospace telemetry vs generous 120px editorial gutters.
3. **Typography hierarchies provide independent spatial geometry**: massive headline offsets vs numbered technical manifests.

## 3. Structural Variance Breakdown in Monochrome

### 3.1 Spatial Silhouettes Observed in Monochrome
1. **Asymmetric Split Canvas (`layout-asymmetric-split`)**: Clear lateral dividing axis separating sticky author dossier from right-hand scrolling runway.
2. **Narrow Reading Monograph (`layout-narrow-reading-column`)**: Off-center, editorial measure with generous asymmetric left indentation.
3. **Full-Bleed Work Runway (`layout-edge-to-edge-editorial`)**: Expansive full-width section headers with immediate project launch cards.
4. **Command Console Matrix (`layout-command-console`)**: Distinct boxed terminal surfaces with dashed border delimiters and ASCII status indicators.
5. **Horizontal Gallery Exhibition (`layout-image-gallery`)**: Clear horizontal track containers with visual peek boundaries.
6. **3-Column Magazine Spread (`layout-magazine`)**: Multi-column editorial distribution with headline hierarchy spanning 2 columns.
7. **Spatial Orbit 3D Stage (`layout-floating-spatial`)**: Fullscreen hero stage with centered focal title followed by orbiting artifact docks.
8. **Chronological Milestone Spine (`layout-timeline`)**: Continuous vertical timeline spine with alternating milestone cards.
