Aesthetic Engineering

Interfaces as physical optical materials

Software has spent thirty years emulating dry paper and flat plastic. My work treats pixels as laminar optical fluids: surfaces governed by wave dispersion, surface tension, and spectral index refraction.

When a user interacts with a control, the response should not be an arbitrary linear opacity transition. It should radiate, ripple, and refract ambient light like obsidian stone washed in liquid chrome.

Laminar Wave Dispersion

Evaluating parallel scalar field equations to maintain pristine laminar flow without turbulent noise artifacts.

Sub-Pixel Chromatic Fringing

Decomposing highlights across red, green, and blue wavelengths to emulate authentic optical prism dispersion.

Zero-Latency Haptic Ripple

Instantaneous multi-point ripple propagation mapped directly to hardware trackpad and touchscreen inputs.
