TECHNOLOGY

How Our AI Builds Your Portfolio

MyFolio's design intelligence engine analyzes your GitHub repositories (languages, stars, commit patterns, descriptions), your resume's content hierarchy, and your career answers to select the optimal 3D visual universe and information architecture for your unique profile.

It then generates bespoke HTML, CSS, and Three.js code with your real projects, actual skills, and genuine work history. No placeholder content. No generic cards. Your portfolio is truly yours.
