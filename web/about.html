MyFolio's design intelligence pipeline analyzes your GitHub repositories (commit cadences, language clusters, star counts, descriptions), your resume's content hierarchy, and career goals to select the optimal 3D visual universe and layout geometry.

It then compiles bespoke HTML, CSS, and Three.js WebGL shaders with your real projects, actual stack, and genuine engineering milestones. No placeholder templates.
