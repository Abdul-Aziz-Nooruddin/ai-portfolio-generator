Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 31, 2026

At MyFolio ("we", "our", or "us"), accessible from https://myfolio.tech, we prioritize the confidentiality and security of our users' personal information. This Privacy Policy details the types of data we collect, how it is processed, and your rights under global privacy regulations including GDPR and CCPA.

1. Information We Collect

To generate and host 3D developer portfolios, we process the following information:

  • Account Information: Full name, email address, username, and encrypted password credentials.
  • GitHub Public Metadata: Public repository names, descriptions, primary languages, commit heatmaps, and profile bios retrieved via public GitHub API endpoints.
  • Resume & Profile Documents: Work history, skills, education, and contact links submitted for portfolio generation.
  • Telemetry & Analytics: Anonymized visitor counts, portfolio pageviews, browser user agents, and IP addresses hashed for fraud defense.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To synthesize bespoke 3D WebGL developer portfolios and interactive live themes.
  • To host and serve your custom domain and subdomains on global edge CDNs.
  • To prevent brute-force attacks, abusive traffic, and malicious API exploitation.
  • To notify you about critical portfolio status updates, custom domain SSL verification, and account notices.

3. Data Retention and Deletion Rights

You have full ownership of your data. You may request permanent deletion of your account, published portfolios, and uploaded resume assets at any time via your Account Dashboard or by contacting us directly.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

We use essential HTTP-only cookies exclusively for maintaining your secure authentication session. We also use browser local storage to preserve your theme previews and weekly generation allowance meters.
