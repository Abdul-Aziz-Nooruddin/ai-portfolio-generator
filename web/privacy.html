LEGAL & PRIVACY PRACTICES

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026 • Effective Date: Immediately

1. The Foundation of Our Privacy Philosophy

MyFolio was built on a simple principle: your professional work is yours. We treat your repositories, projects, and personal data with the same respect and security we demand for our own code.

Core Guarantee: We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal information or portfolio telemetry to third-party data brokers, ad networks, or recruiters.

2. What Information We Access and Collect

To generate and publish your portfolio, MyFolio accesses only what is necessary:

  • Public GitHub Metadata: Public repository names, primary programming languages, star counts, commit dates, and README summaries. We never ask for or access private repositories without explicit authorization.
  • Account Coordinates: Your email address and authentication tokens used to secure your portfolio and notify you of deployment status.
  • User-Supplied Content: Custom biographies, chosen showcase links, uploaded images, and project stories edited in Web Studio.
  • Technical Logs: IP addresses and basic request headers needed to protect against distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) attacks and ensure 99.9% uptime via Cloudflare.

3. How Your Information is Handled

Your data is used strictly to power your portfolio:

  • Parsing your public achievements to generate tailored 3D scene nodes and WebGL stages.
  • Compiling static, edge-cached single-page portfolios bound to your custom domain or sub-path.
  • Sending essential operational notifications (e.g. domain verification results, security alerts).

4. Email Communication Security

All direct inquiries sent to our corporate communication channels (support@myfolio.tech and aziz@myfolio.tech) are transmitted over TLS encryption and handled via Titan Business Email infrastructure with strict SPF, DKIM, and DMARC verification.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

We use local storage and essential session tokens exclusively for core functionality: preserving your Web Studio theme preferences, maintaining authenticated session state, and remembering your cookie consent preference. We do not deploy intrusive third-party cross-site tracking cookies.

6. Sovereign Portability & Deletion

You have full ownership of your portfolio content. At any time, you may:

  • Export a standalone ZIP archive containing all compiled HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and WebGL assets to host anywhere.
  • Request complete permanent deletion of your account, generated sites, and metadata from our servers by contacting support@myfolio.tech.

7. Questions & Contact

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal data, reach out directly:

Technical & Privacy Support: support@myfolio.tech
Founder Direct: aziz@myfolio.tech
